Visitor Info

Know before you go. Find helpful information for planning your visit to Taniti.

Money

The U.S. dollar is Taniti's currency, but many businesses also accept euros and yen. Major credit cards are widely accepted.

Electricity

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. English is less common in rural areas, especially among older residents.

Health & Safety

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. Violent crime is rare, although visitors should be aware of increasing reports of petty theft.

Local Laws

The drinking age is 18. Alcohol cannot be sold or served between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

Holidays

Taniti celebrates many national holidays. Some tourist attractions and restaurants may be closed on holidays.
